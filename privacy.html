Privacy Policy

What We Collect

Cybernetic Strategy Universe does not require an account and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, payment information, or other direct personal information through this site. The site does not provide public forms, comments, or user-upload features.

Hosting Logs

Our hosting provider may process standard technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and timestamps, to deliver and secure the site. We do not use that information to identify visitors ourselves.

Cookies and Analytics

This site does not intentionally use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or an analytics platform. Your browser or third-party services may still apply their own technologies when you visit linked services.

Third-Party Services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and may link to Steam. Those services may receive technical information when your browser connects to them. Please review their respective privacy policies for details.

Children

This site is not directed at collecting information from children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site or its services change. The latest version will always be posted on this page with its updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the Cybernetic Strategy project team through its official project channel.
